Every API your device uses

None of them keeps access logs. Your IP address reaches them, as with any website, and is only held in memory to limit how many requests one client can make. It is never stored.

The appnullifex.fun/app
checking

Serves the app itself: its pages, its code and the proving files. Static files, the same for everyone.

Receives
Requests for those files.
Never sees
Anything you type or hold. The app runs on your device, and your keys never leave it.

The extension carries all of this inside its own package.

Solana gatewayrpc.nullifex.fun
checking

Reads Solana for the app: balances, transaction status and live updates.

Receives
The Solana requests the app makes, such as a balance lookup.
Never sees
Your keys, or which notes are yours.

The Solana node behind it sees Nullifex’s server, never your IP address, your browser or the page you’re on.

Relayerrelayer.nullifex.fun
checking

Submits your private transactions and pays their network fee. Quotes fees, trades and NullifexSwap routes.

Receives
Each transaction with its proof, and quote requests: tokens, amounts, and a withdrawal’s destination. For NullifexSwap, the destination chain, recipient and amount.
Never sees
Your keys or your balance. It can’t change where funds go: your proof fixes the recipient and the fee.
Indexerindexer.nullifex.fun
checking

Serves public on-chain data, the same for everyone: encrypted notes, the Merkle tree, and token names, prices and logos.

Receives
Requests for that public data.
Never sees
Which notes are yours. Your device finds them by decrypting, and checks the data against the root the Nullifex program records on-chain.

Nothing else runs in your browser

No third-party code

Every script comes from Nullifex’s own server or the extension package. No analytics, ads, tag managers or chat widgets.

No outside fonts or images

Fonts are Nullifex’s own files. Token logos come through Nullifex’s indexer, so the sites that host them never see you.

No cookies, no accounts

Nothing to sign up for, and nothing that follows you from site to site.

No remote code in the extension

Everything it runs is inside the package Chrome reviewed. Its only permission is storage.

Where outside services come in

Some work needs the wider network. Nullifex’s servers do it for you, so these services hear from Nullifex, not from you.

A Solana nodeReading Solana and sending transactions, for the gateway and the relayerNullifex’s server. Your requests arrive without your IP address, browser or origin.
JupiterTrade routes, lending markets, the token list and pricesNullifex’s server, and the trade or position being quoted, which becomes public on Solana anyway.

Your own choices stay yours: a wallet you connect, such as Phantom, is its own software with its own connections. Links you open, such as Solscan or X, take you to those sites. And anything you shield, withdraw or send to a deposit address is public on Solana, like any Solana transaction.

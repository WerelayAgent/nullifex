Nullifex is live on Solana mainnet

Nullifex is a privacy layer for Solana. Shield any token into a private balance, then send, trade, lend and withdraw without a public trail back to your wallet. Starting today it runs on mainnet.

Update, October 6: the on-chain program has been audited by ContractWolf: 95/100, every finding resolved.

Open the app →How it works

What you can do today

  • Shield SOL, USDC, USDT or any other SPL / Token-2022 token into a private balance. It takes one wallet signature, and it is the last public trace of you.
  • Send privately to any Nullifex address. Amounts stay hidden, and when the fee is paid in private SOL, so does the token.
  • Trade inside your private balance. The Nullifex vault swaps through Jupiter, so you get its routing across Solana DEXes without your wallet appearing in the trade.
  • Earn by lending privately through Jupiter Lend.
  • Withdraw to any address, or to a fresh one-time address only your vault controls.

Inside Nullifex you never sign with your wallet. A relayer pays the network fee and submits each proof, and it never learns whose notes they are.

How it works, briefly

Your private balance is a set of encrypted notes. Every action is a zero-knowledge proof (Groth16 on BN254) built in your browser, and the Nullifex program on Solana checks it before any funds move. Your keys are created and encrypted in the browser and never reach our servers. The indexer and relayer never see your keys or which notes are yours.

No deposit limits

Nullifex launched with per-token limits. Since its audit by ContractWolf they're lifted: no token has a per-deposit limit or a vault cap.

Anyone can open a vault for any other token. A newly opened vault starts with default limits until the multisig lifts them.

Fees: Nullifex takes 0.3% on shield, withdraw and swap, and sends it to the multisig treasury. Private sends carry only the relayer fee, which covers the network and priority fee and the one-time record each spent note needs. Every fee is shown before you confirm.

Where security stands

External audit: the on-chain program was audited by ContractWolf (October 6, 2026; 95/100, all findings resolved). Alongside it:

  • 522 automated checks across 6 suites, including real proofs replayed against the on-chain program and 27 attack scenarios it must refuse.
  • Internal adversarial reviews of the program, circuit, app and servers. All fixes are live, the program's through the audited, multisig-approved upgrade of October 6.
  • End-to-end runs on a copy of mainnet with the real Jupiter and Jupiter Lend programs, then on mainnet itself.

Next: a verifiable build of the program. Found something? info@nullifex.fun. Live status is on the security page.

Who's building it

Nullifex is developed by ex CLI and GUI team members at Monero. There they were in charge of improving wallet synchronization, node setup and overall usability, and made systems faster, simpler and more reliable while improving wallet recovery, privacy explanations and hardware-wallet support. Nullifex brings that experience to Solana.

Try it

Connect Phantom (or any Solana wallet), create your vault, and shield a little SOL. Save your recovery key: it's the only way to restore your private balance.

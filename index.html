How it works

Five steps. One trace.

  1. Every Solana wallet is a public ledger. Anyone can trace who you pay.

  2. One Phantom approval moves funds into Nullifex's shared vault. The last public trace of you.

  3. Your balance becomes encrypted notes that only your browser can open.

  4. Trades settle in batches from the vault, signed by a relayer. The pool only ever sees Nullifex.

  5. Withdraw to a fresh address. Where it came from stays sealed.

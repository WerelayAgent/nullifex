Prove your history in three steps

Your viewing key lets anyone you choose read your Nullifex history and check it against the chain. It can’t spend.

01Open your vault

In the app, go to Keys. Your viewing key is there, next to your receiving address.

02Copy your viewing key

It is read-only. Share it only with the person who needs it: an accountant, an auditor, an exchange’s compliance team.

03They check it

The key opens your notes, which can then be matched to the public entries and exits on Solana, without your spending key. The app can’t open a viewing key yet: for now that takes Nullifex’s client code or our help.

Safeguards in the protocol

Enforced by the program on Solana, not by policy alone.

Public entry and exit

Every shield and withdrawal is an ordinary, visible Solana transaction. Value can’t enter or leave Nullifex unseen.

Risky tokens refused

Tokens with features that could endanger a shared vault are rejected on-chain when a vault is opened.

Issuer controls respected

Freezable tokens such as USDC keep their issuer’s controls over the vault. The app labels them.

Emergency pause

A 2-of-2 multisig can pause the protocol while an issue is investigated. No instruction lets it move or redirect funds.

Every rule enforced on-chain

Amounts, recipients and fees are bound into each zero-knowledge proof and checked by the program before anything moves.

Acceptable use

Financial privacy is a normal expectation. Using Nullifex to break the law is not permitted.

Nullifex is built for
  • Keeping your salary, savings and balances out of public view
  • Paying people without exposing your whole history
  • Trading and lending without broadcasting your positions
  • Protecting yourself from being tracked or targeted
  • Showing your records to the people you choose
Not permitted
  • Moving the proceeds of crime or hiding their origin
  • Evading sanctions, or use by any sanctioned person or entity
  • Financing terrorism or any other illegal activity
  • Fraud, scams or theft of any kind
  • Use where it is prohibited by the laws that apply to you

By using Nullifex you confirm you are not a sanctioned person or acting for one, and that you will meet your own legal and tax obligations.

Requests from authorities

What Nullifex can do
  • Explain how the protocol works and what is public on-chain
  • Pause the protocol through the multisig while an issue is investigated
  • Point to the public shield and withdrawal transactions, which anyone can read
What Nullifex can’t do
  • Read anyone’s balance or history: it never has the keys
  • Freeze, seize or move an individual’s funds
  • Produce logs of who used the relayer: none are kept

Questions

How is Nullifex different from a mixer?

Nullifex is a private balance you keep and use: you shield once, then send, trade and lend inside it. Entry and exit stay public, every action is checked on-chain, and your full history remains provable with your viewing key.

Can Nullifex freeze, seize or move my funds?

No. The program has no instruction that moves a user’s notes without their proof. The admin multisig can pause deposits, transfers and swaps while an issue is investigated; withdrawals stay open. In an incident it can also pause everything leaving the pool for at most about 3 days, ending by itself, with at least 7 days between two such pauses. No instruction lets it single out, freeze or take anyone’s balance. The same multisig controls program upgrades, so it could change these rules through an upgrade; upgrades are announced in advance.

Can Nullifex hand over my transaction history?

Nullifex doesn’t have it. Notes are encrypted to your keys in your browser, and the relayer and indexer keep no record of who asked for what. Your viewing key is the only way to read your history, and only you can share it.

How do I show an exchange where my funds came from?

Give their compliance team your viewing key. It shows every note you received and spent, which they can match to the public shield and withdrawal transactions on Solana.

Is it legal to use Nullifex where I live?

Rules on privacy tools differ by country and change over time. You are responsible for following the laws that apply to you, including tax reporting. If you are unsure, ask a qualified adviser.
